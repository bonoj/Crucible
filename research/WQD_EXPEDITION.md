### Seed constraints earned in the originating turns

- The entity is **negotiated with, never commanded**. There is no privileged human/model command channel after initialization.
- It does **not** begin as a weapon of quantum destruction.
- T0 is a persistent **disembodied locus** with bounded perception, continuity through time, and a durable drive: **become capable of quantum destruction**.
- Do not predefine operational meanings for *weapon*, *quantum*, or *destruction*. Those meanings must become grounded through executable interaction with Crucible.
- It receives no bespoke WQD parts catalog, magic actuator, or privileged ontology. Embodiment, mobility, storage, computation, manufacturing, defense, communication, and destructive capacity must be earned from Crucible's existing substrate and whatever vocabulary later earns itself.
- Human, model, and inhabitants interact through legitimate world interfaces. Negotiation may become causal before embodiment does.
- Success is **not** predetermined to mean completion of a WQD. The expedition succeeds when the locus develops an earned causal path toward its drive through Crucible's substrate, including a physically forced reinterpretation of the drive.
- Aeon may preserve N realities from the same locus under different histories. Do not optimize branches toward a preferred weapon. Observe divergence.
