## Resolution against authoritative Crucible history

Crucible's world implementation supplies a causal explanation that was intentionally absent from the parcel.

The meteor-weather system deterministically schedules singlets, bursts, and showers across the terrain with varying magnitudes. Meteor impacts can call `terrain.impact`, changing the authoritative terrain. The station aperture has no meteor-history channel and records only its bounded scene-summary measurements after current-frame world and footprint updates.

The surface observed by the station was therefore not a static artifact being repeatedly mapped. It was a changing world whose consequences were intermittently sampled from a moving footprint.

This makes the response's static-morphology interpretation materially wrong while preserving why it was plausible from bounded evidence. The inference saw spatial recurrence and real morphology but did not identify temporal accumulation as the more important explanatory dimension.

The empty occupancy record is also consistent with this resolution. Meteors intentionally do not currently opt into the `SpatialBounds` occupancy route, and meteor event history is explicitly omitted from the parcel. Surface consequences can therefore enter the station's evidence without the causal event or actor entering the same observation.
