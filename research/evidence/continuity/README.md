# Continuity crossing evidence

This directory preserves primary evidence produced by the orbital-locus continuity expedition.

## Crossing

A **crossing** is one bounded handoff from Crucible evidence to an independently instantiated inference, followed by preservation of that inference's response before interpretation or comparison with authoritative world state.

For this expedition, an independently instantiated inference means a model invocation that is not given the ongoing Crucible collaboration context. The provider may retain or apply its own ordinary product-level context; that is not treated as a known model-internal reset. The experimental claim is narrower: the invocation receives no expedition context from us beyond the frozen parcel and the neutral handoff prompt.

A crossing is not evidence that the underlying model is stateless, memoryless, newly initialized, or otherwise internally isolated. Those properties are not assumed.
