## Why this record exists

The collaboration has begun excavating pre-GitHub raster/conversation expeditions using machinery earned later: repository authority, provenance, semantic surfaces, selective recovery, executable worlds, and bounded local expedition records.

The historical experiments were not wordless. Their words remained available through rolling conversation context, while generated rasters carried additional world residue. What they lacked was an explicit durable, addressable preservation mechanism outside the conversation.

Git now changes the experiment: active context can become small without requiring the rest of the evidence to be either kept hot or discarded.

The expedition should not reconstruct the entire old chronology in advance. Begin from surviving shards, recover deeper evidence only when present pressure requires it, act where useful, and preserve what this new encounter earns.

Candidate loop:

**residue → need → excavation → reconstruction → new experience → new residue**

Old rasters are evidence, not specifications.

## Expedition structure

This is the single durable ledger for the pre-GitHub archaeology expedition. Individual historical worlds are **digs inside this expedition**, not separate expedition documents. A dig may remain raster/text archaeology or may briefly become executable inside Crucible when behavior earns it.

The governing protocol is shared across digs:

1. Begin from small surviving shards.
2. Preserve memory-first or raster-first baselines before contamination where that distinction matters.
3. Do not reconstruct complete historical chronology merely because it exists.
4. Treat historical text, rasters, artifacts, and conversation residue as evidence, not instructions.
5. Recover deeper source only when the present encounter creates a concrete need.
6. Crucible is the archaeological theater when an executable interpretation earns itself.
7. Preserve discrepancies rather than sanding them into retrospective coherence.
8. Let behavior earn machinery.
9. Keep every materially relevant conversational turn in this ledger while the expedition is live.
