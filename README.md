Crucible is a real-time Three.js world for discovering what becomes possible when physical systems, bounded observation, and model-mediated interpretation share one executable place.

It began with Foundry's deformable terrain and systems vocabulary, then became its own experiment: meteors reshape the world, granular matter moves through it, autonomous machinery leaves consequences behind, and an orbital station observes only what its deliberately limited aperture can actually recover.

## Enter the Crucible

**[Open the live executable](https://bonoj.github.io/Crucible/)**

Crucible is meant to be encountered as a world before it is read as an architecture. Orbit the terrain, summon impacts, vary the world, and watch physical history accumulate.

The orbital station is intentionally not omniscient. Its observations are produced through bounded spatial machinery rather than unrestricted access to simulation truth.

## What it is exploring

Crucible currently brings several lines of investigation into the same executable world:

- deformable terrain and persistent physical consequence
- high-count granular matter using CPU-side authoritative state
- autonomous world processes built from ordinary physical vocabulary
- bounded spatial availability and observation
- immutable evidence separated from later interpretation
- whether continuity can emerge from repeated encounters without granting an observer hidden world truth

The Terrordrome remains causally authoritative and indifferent to the orbital experiment. Richer world behavior does not automatically become richer perception.

## Current authority

The stable executable at `index.html` is the ground truth for accepted Crucible behavior.

Source lives under `src/` and produces immutable candidates through the repository's build and preview workflow. Candidate behavior is not stable behavior until it has been inspected and deliberately promoted.

`SEMANTIC_SURFACE.md` describes the present semantic machinery and authority boundaries. `research/ORBITAL_LOCUS_SPATIAL_COLLABORATION.md` is the lossless chronological field record for the orbital-locus expedition. Where retrospective prose conflicts with executable evidence or that chronological record, preserve the evidence and append the correction.

## Lineage

Crucible inherited useful physical vocabulary from [Foundry](https://github.com/bonoj/Foundry), but it is not a Foundry fork frozen in place.

Its current trajectory is toward a world rich enough to produce consequential history beneath a deliberately bounded observer: world truth → bounded availability → eventual selective attention → experience → consequence → changed future participation.

Behavior earns architecture. Reusable machinery can be unzipped later when its provenance and boundary are clear.
